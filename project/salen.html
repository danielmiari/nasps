Project

Scandic - Sälen

Scandic is expanding in Sweden with a new hotel in Sälen featuring 120 rooms, 16 apartments, and conference facilities, set to open in the 2027/2028 season. The hotel will offer a prime ski-in/ski-out location along with a wellness area including a sauna, gym, and pool. The investment is part of Scandic’s strategy to strengthen its position in the Nordic market. It also responds to growing demand from both winter and summer tourism.
