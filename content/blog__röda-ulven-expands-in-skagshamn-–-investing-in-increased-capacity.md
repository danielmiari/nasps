# Röda Ulven expands in Skagshamn – investing in increased capacity

Posted on

May 13, 2026

Posted by

Kim Rasmussen - Co Founder

Nordic Anchor & Steel Pile Supply AB (NASPS AB) is proud to contribute to the development of Röda Ulven, which continues to grow its production in Skagshamn through a planned expansion of the existing facility.

Nordic Anchor & Steel Pile Supply AB (NASPS AB) is proud to contribute to the development of Röda Ulven, which continues to grow its production in Skagshamn through a planned expansion of the existing facility.

The project comprises a new cold storage facility of approximately 500 m², intended to strengthen the company's capacity to handle variations in raw material supply and to ensure a stable supply of surströmming to the market. The investment is an important step in future-proofing production within an industry that in recent years has been marked by limited availability of herring.

The expansion takes place directly adjacent to the existing factory in Skagshamn and is a strategic investment to optimize storage, logistics and quality throughout the entire production chain.

In the project's foundation phase, NASPS AB has supplied 75 R51/29 anchors, contributing to a safe and efficient foundation for the new facility.

The delivery was carried out in collaboration with Nordisk Grundteknik AB as drilling contractor. NASPS AB thanks them for the order and looks forward to further collaborations.
