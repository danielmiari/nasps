# Nordic Anchor & Steel Pile Supply AB (NASPS AB) is proud to contribute to the development of a new mountain hotel in Sälen

Posted on

Apr 30, 2024

Posted by

Kim Rasmussen - Co Founder

NASPS delivers high-quality products for geotechnical foundation work in complex environments. Our self-drilling anchor systems enable fast installation and high reliability, even under challenging ground conditions.

Nordic Anchor & Steel Pile Supply AB (NASPS AB) is proud to contribute to the development of a new mountain hotel in Sälen, with Scandic Hotels Group as the operator.

The project involves the construction of a modern hotel with approximately 120 rooms, apartments and associated facilities in an attractive ski-in/ski-out location between Tandådalen and Hundfjället.

As part of the foundation works, NASPS has supplied 85 T76/51 self-drilling anchors (SDA) to the project. The delivery supports an efficient and safe installation in the geotechnically challenging conditions of the mountain environment.

The delivery was carried out in collaboration with Nordisk Pål och Borrteknik AB as drilling contractor. NASPS AB thanks them for the order and looks forward to further collaborations.
