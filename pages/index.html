Nordic Anchor & Steel Pile Supply AB - NASPS

Specialized products for modern foundation engineering.

Nordic Anchor & Steel Pile Supply AB (NASPS) is a specialized supplier of engineered systems and components for foundation construction, geotechnical works, and infrastructure applications. We provide self-drilling anchors, steel pile and interlock solutions, grouting equipment, drilling tools, and associated accessories for contractors operating in technically demanding ground conditions across the Nordic region and Europe. Our product portfolio is selected and developed with focus on structural performance, installation efficiency, dimensional accuracy, durability, and site-proven functionality. NASPS supports projects where reliable load transfer, efficient drilling performance, controlled grouting, and robust execution methods are critical to achieving safe and cost-effective construction outcomes.

"Through our work in foundation, engineering and infrastructure support, we help create safer communities and more resilient built environments.
