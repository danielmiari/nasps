The self-drilling anchoring system that NASPS supplies has completed EPD registration. Environmental data based on a life cycle assessment is now documented and publicly registered for the product.

The self-drilling anchoring system that NASPS supplies has completed EPD registration. Environmental data based on a life cycle assessment is now documented and publicly registered for the product.

An Environmental Product Declaration, EPD, reports a product’s environmental performance in a standardised way, with a life cycle assessment as its basis. Because every declaration follows the same set of rules, the figures can be compared between products and used directly in a project’s own environmental calculations.

The declaration is registered by our supplier Sinorock and covers their self-drilling anchoring system under registration number EPD-IES-0031637_001.

For our customers this means the documentation is there when a project asks for it: in procurement with environmental requirements, in the client’s environmental assessment, and as input to the climate declaration required for new buildings in Sweden.

Do you need the environmental documentation for the anchors in a specific project? Get in touch and we will send it over.

Source: Sinorock, “Sinorock Self-Drilling Anchoring System Completes EPD Registration”, published on 18 September 2026 on sinorockco.com.
